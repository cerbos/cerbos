type:fix
Evaluate policy version conditions in query plans instead of denying
The query planner dropped the principal and resource policy versions from the plan request and could not resolve fields by their JSON names on messages nested under `request`. A condition such as `request.principal.policyVersion == request.resource.policyVersion` failed to evaluate and the plan denied the action, although `CheckResources` allowed it. The planner now evaluates these conditions, and treats the resource policy version as known like the resource kind and scope, so `R.policyVersion` no longer appears in plan filters.
